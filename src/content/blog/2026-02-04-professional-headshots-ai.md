## Why People Don't Update Their Headshots

It's not vanity. It's friction. A professional photography session requires:

- Finding and booking a photographer with availability
- Figuring out locations, outfits, prep
- The session itself — usually 1–3 hours
- Waiting 1–2 weeks for the edited gallery
- Paying $1,500–$3,000 for the privilege
- Doing it all again in 18 months when the photos date themselves

For a solo attorney, a real estate agent, a consultant, or an entrepreneur who's already at capacity — that's a lot. So the LinkedIn photo stays from 2019, and the website headshot stays from the previous job. It's not ideal, but it's not urgent enough to deal with.
